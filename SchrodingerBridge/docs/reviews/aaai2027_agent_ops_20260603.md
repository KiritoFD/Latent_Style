## Review-lane separation rule

The standing lanes are not the same thing as a full formal review cycle.

The standing lanes:

1. maintain continuity in literature, theory, remote execution, and adversarial
   pressure;
2. surface evidence gaps early;
3. reduce silent drift between code, experiments, and paper wording.

Formal review closure still requires independent review-cycle evidence as
tracked in:

- `docs/reviews/aaai2027_review_registry.csv`
